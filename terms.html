Terms of Service

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Blox War Stories is a small creator tool that uploads short history videos, made by the Blox War Stories channel, to the channel's own TikTok account.

1. Who can use it

The tool is used only by the owner of the Blox War Stories channel to post their own videos. It is not offered to the public.

2. What it does

After the owner signs in with TikTok and approves access, the tool uploads finished videos with a title, caption and hashtags the owner has approved. It never posts to any account other than the one that authorized it.

3. Content

All videos are original works created by the channel. The owner is responsible for making sure every video follows TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

4. Removing access

The owner can disconnect the tool from TikTok at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

5. No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without any guarantee that uploads will always succeed.
