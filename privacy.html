Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Blox War Stories is a small creator tool that uploads the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account.

What we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (user.info.basic), used only to confirm which account is connected before posting.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish) to the connected account.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect information about other TikTok users.
  • We don't sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • We don't read the account's messages, followers or other private data.

Storage

The sign-in token is held by the connection service (Composio) so the tool can post on the owner's request. No other TikTok data is stored.

Your control

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions). After that, the tool can no longer post or read anything.

Children

The tool is used only by the channel owner and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
